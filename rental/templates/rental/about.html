Our Story

Where Tradition Meets Modern Elegance

Founded in 2023 in the vibrant heart of Hargeisa, Somaliland, Royal Rental Suits began with a simple yet powerful vision: to make premium formalwear accessible to every man who wants to feel confident on his special day.

What started as a carefully curated collection of 30 premium suits has rapidly grown into one of the most trusted rental destinations in the city. We proudly serve hundreds of clients each year for weddings, graduations, corporate events, and formal occasions across Somaliland.

  • Carefully selected premium suits from top global brands
  • Expert styling and personalized fitting guidance
  • Strict quality control and professional dry cleaning
  • A dedicated team focused on exceptional customer care
